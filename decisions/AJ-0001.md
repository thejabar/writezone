Title

Why WriteZone Chose an Engine Architecture

Decision

Introduce an Engine layer between Services and Models, with a shared Processor contract and Pipeline infrastructure.

Reason

To allow future capabilities—such as ranking, discovery, reputation, moderation, and AI—to evolve independently without forcing changes to Controllers or Views.

Outcome

The application transitioned from a traditional MVC structure to a capability-driven architecture while maintaining full backward compatibility.

Long-term Impact

Every future engine will inherit the same architectural language.
