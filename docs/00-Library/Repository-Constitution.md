WriteZone Library

Repository Constitution

Version 1.0

“Code explains implementation. The Library explains intention.”

Welcome to the WriteZone Library.

This directory is not traditional documentation. It is the architectural and philosophical foundation of the platform.

Before contributing code, every engineer, designer, researcher, or AI contributor is expected to understand the principles documented here.

The Library exists to preserve not only how WriteZone works, but why it was designed that way.

⸻

The Four Questions

Every document inside this Library answers one of four questions:

Why?

Why does WriteZone exist?

What principles guide every decision?

What problems are we trying to solve?

These answers live inside the Manifesto.

⸻

What?

What experience are we creating?

What should users feel?

How should the product evolve?

These answers live inside the Product documents.

⸻

How?

How is the platform engineered?

How do the engines communicate?

How do we keep the architecture maintainable?

These answers live inside the Architecture and Engineering documents.

⸻

Where?

Where is WriteZone going?

Which engines will exist?

How will AI fit into the platform?

These answers live inside the Blueprints and Vision documents.

⸻

The WriteZone Rule

No major engine is implemented before its blueprint exists.

No blueprint is approved until it passes the WriteZone Test.

No feature exists without purpose.

⸻

Repository Philosophy

The platform and the Library evolve together.

Every important engineering decision is documented.

Every architecture decision is intentional.

Every product decision strengthens the mission.

The Library is considered a first-class part of the WriteZone repository.

Protect it with the same care as the codebase.

Because software changes.

Principles endure.
